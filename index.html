The room

Checker floor. Marble tables. Brass overhead.

Steel beams painted butter yellow, caged brass pendants, a botanical mural drawn straight onto the wall — the room does most of the talking. Seating spans a full floor with more upstairs.

Our story

Fresh Food.
Fresh Coffee.

Café du L'Amour — Cafe of Love — is a Bengaluru-born café run by H-Square Business Ventures LLP. Our cooking is built around a Continental foundation with an Indian understanding: contemporary, a little global, but never a stretch for the Indian palate.

Everything is made fresh — food, coffee and the everyday running of the place — which is the whole philosophy in four words: Fresh Food, Fresh Coffee.

Also at Century Club

The same kitchen independently runs the Continental food-service counter at Century Club, Bengaluru — serving members and guests across the main dining area, ancillary spaces and the Party House.
